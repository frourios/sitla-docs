# Commercial Model

*Informational business description — not part of SITLA Protocol conformance*  
**Date:** November 14, 2026

This page describes only the [SITLA operator deployment](sitla-operator.md). Independent operators choose their own fees, payment providers and commercial agreements under the [Operator Profile](protocol-operators.md).

## 1. What the Fee Pays For

*SITLA earns an intermediation fee for circulation. Evidence assurance is not purchased with the fee.*

The service intermediates discovery and authorized circulation of requests, data, credential services, presentations and payments. It does not sell a guarantee of nationality, qualification, truthful evidence or successful physical work. The [Marketplace Profile](protocol-marketplace.md) defines the technical payment boundary; Core Section 2 defines evidence limits.

## 2. Commercial Baseline

*These numbers describe the business model, not interoperability requirements.*

For supported paid work transactions, the current commercial baseline allocates **20% to SITLA and 80% to the seller**. SITLA bears payment-provider processing fees; its 20% is gross fee revenue before those expenses. Free discovery is not made billable merely because the architecture includes discovery among its functions. Exact fee scope, rounding, tax presentation, exceptions, cancellation and refund treatment must be disclosed for the actual product/transaction.

Changing these commercial conditions does not change a circuit, an evidence level or technical protocol conformance. No charge can authorize a credential issuer to assert facts it has not accepted. Institution participation and any permitted fee/intermediary arrangement require that institution's approval.

## 3. Publication Boundary

*Use the transaction's actual terms when deciding to pay.*

This draft is not a substitute for the [terms](terms.md), [privacy policy](privacy.md), payment-provider agreement or a specific quote/contract. It does not announce support for task escrow, institutional verification, milestone settlement or any unimplemented feature. The protocol and document publication do not themselves execute, capture or guarantee a payment.
